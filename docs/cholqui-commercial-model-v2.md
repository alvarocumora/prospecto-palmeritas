# Cholqui Commercial Model v2

## Objective

Separate commercial evidence from planning assumptions and prevent unverified logistics or inventory from being presented as decision-grade economics.

## Production-plan control

The source model declares **166,500 t in 24 months**. The current mathematical ramp from 1,000 t/month to a 15,000 t/month plateau over 12 months produces **270,167 t**, a difference of **103,667 t (+62.3%)**.

The declared 166,500 t are also **2.74×** the 60,750 t conceptual geological potential currently documented. Neither figure is a mineral resource or reserve.

The platform therefore keeps three concepts separate:

- **Declared plan:** 166,500 t / 24 months.
- **Mathematical ramp:** 270,167 t / 24 months under the existing ramp function.
- **Reconciled scenario:** the ramp may be capped at 166,500 t for sensitivity analysis, but the cap is not evidence that the inventory exists.

## Freight

The legacy model uses:

- 130 km
- CLP 90/t·km
- CLP 11,700/t

This is retained only as a **planning assumption**. It is not represented as a verified road distance or a current carrier quote.

Sensitivity rates are 60 / 90 / 120 CLP/t·km. Once a route distance is verified, the platform can calculate freight sensitivity without changing the provenance of the input.

## Mine netback

The commercial comparison is based on:

`mine netback = gross settlement - freight - acid adjustment - moisture adjustment - penalties - other commercial costs`

CEMIN and ENAMI should be compared on **mine netback**, not on distance alone.

## Decision-grade gate

Payback is not a decision-grade KPI until all of the following are verified:

1. Mineable inventory supporting the commercial plan.
2. Metallurgical recovery / acid consumption / variability.
3. Product acceptance at the selected destination.
4. Road distance for the actual haul route.
5. Freight quote from a carrier.
6. Settlement/tariff quote and commercial deductions.

The numerical payback generated by the engineering scenario may be retained internally as sensitivity, but the user interface must label the commercial case **PENDING_DD** and not present that payback as an investment KPI.


## Logistics benchmark — September 2026

Current external references used by the engineering model:

- RM diesel average: **CLP 1,392/L** (25 Sep 2026, CNE-derived published reference).
- 20 m3 8x4 mining dump-truck rental: **from CLP 6.5M/month** (published rental reference; project conditions to quote).
- 14 m3 dump-truck service: **CLP 3,000/km** (2026 public procurement award; not a Cholqui quote).
- Payload used for comparison: **28 t/trip**.
- Operating calendar: **26 days/month**.
- Productivity: **1.5 round trips/truck/day**.
- Loaded / empty fuel efficiency: **2.6 / 2.8 km/L** engineering assumptions.

At the legacy 130 km one-way planning distance, the model uses a 260 km round trip. The distance is not upgraded to VERIFIED: public route evidence supports the regional order of magnitude, but the exact heavy-truck route from the Cholqui mine gate to Catemu or Los Robles must be routed and field-checked.

The rental comparison includes only **truck rental + diesel**. It deliberately excludes driver, tolls, tyres, maintenance, insurance, mobilisation, waiting time and contractor margin, so it must be treated as a lower-bound operating benchmark.

The third-party comparison applies the public CLP 3,000/km award to the full round trip. It is a market benchmark, not a supplier quotation for Cholqui.
